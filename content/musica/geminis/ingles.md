***geminis*** gathers my brother's music: the first three tracks are by *emi*.

I felt like I had something left to do for him, I needed to put these tracks out. I wanted to have a little corner of the internet where I could go looking for them, **looking for him**.

I barely touched the tracks, mostly adjusting their structures and giving them names. The last one is mine: I made it thinking of him, and I think it captures something of his cadence.

I hope they reach the people who knew him, and that they can be of some comfort to them.
