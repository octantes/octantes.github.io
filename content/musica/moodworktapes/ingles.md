***mood worktapes*** was my first project, made while practicing the fundamentals of Ableton Live. Before this I had never used a DAW, so everything was learned from scratch through experimentation.

The album is built around percussion, synthesizers and rock-influenced electronic song structures, with a focus on dark, rhythmic and dramatic compositions. I still couldn't connect the guitar to the computer, so it's all written and produced in MIDI, using a Launchpad Mini and Ableton's VSTs.

I remember the process as one of total **obsession**: it was all I wanted to do, every day, all day. I have a soft spot for it; having no prior experience or established process forced me to navigate *on intuition alone*.
