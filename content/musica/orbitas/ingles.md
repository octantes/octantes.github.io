***órbitas*** is a collection of tracks that had been accumulating around similar ideas over the years: *loops that come full circle, pyramids from the drawer*.

There is more focus on synthesizers than guitar, although the guitar still appears throughout the album. Some tracks lean toward electronic and video game-like sounds, others are darker. The album as a whole is more energetic and moves through many different places.

Rather than following a single concept or constraint, it works as a kind of sampling of things I made across different periods: a collection of loops, melodies, textures and experiments that happened to find their way into the same record.

### Video

The video explores emergent complexity through fractal-like orbital patterns. It starts with a single orbit, then adds a second, then doubles them to four, eight, sixteen and beyond. As the camera zooms in, it reveals the complex patterns emerging from simple elements.
