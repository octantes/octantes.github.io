*What do you want to change? Shall we read the cards?*

***seance*** went through **many iterations** that I still have saved. Some of them even had finished lyrics, but the vocals never quite clicked. I eventually decided to release it as an instrumental album and move on to the next project.

It was the first project where I could actually connect a guitar to the computer, and the beats were built around processing it as if it were a synthesizer. The tracks use layered, looping structures, gradually adding and removing elements over time, with a subtle hip-hop influence in the way some of the loops and rhythms are put together.

I was figuring out the mixing as I went, experimenting especially with stereo effects and ping-pong delays, so it sounds better on headphones.
