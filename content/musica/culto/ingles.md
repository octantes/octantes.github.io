***culto*** gathers tracks made around 2019, which were actually the first songs I made with my guitar plugged in: *empty rituals, altars of asphalt, and a faith in suspension*.

They stayed in the background for years while I kept thinking about rebuilding and changing a lot of things. Eventually I stopped trying to make them into something else and decided to treat the release as an MVP.

The result is a darker and denser record, centered around guitar, with hip-hop influenced percussion. It has some live drums I played myself, and some little things kinda remind me of nu-metal and trip-hop. It isn't necessarily heavy in a metal sense, but more from the atmosphere, the guitar and the textures.

These are some of the earliest songs I made with guitar, finally getting their place a few years later. Oh, and yes, the visualizer is moving ;)
