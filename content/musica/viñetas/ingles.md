Ideas don't belong to us. They are *the fruit of a collective substrate*: our time and the references we draw from. With that in mind, not sharing what comes from them is an act of individualism, it's not giving back to the world what it gave us.

Perfectionism is **selfish** above all else. In trying to pay tribute to my ideas, to be "worthy of them", I lost my way. The path of art is one of healing, but the cycle isn't complete until we call the work finished: *it doesn't end until we share it*, so we can start again, transformed by the process.

Perfectionism is a mirror that inverts our original purpose: **leading others** along that path. By not sharing, the change stays inside our heads; we carry it within us without ever expressing it outwardly.

***viñetas*** is one of those projects that had been fermenting on my hard drive since 2020. I abandoned it hoping to improve the mix at some later point and moved on to the next project. I got distracted and forgot about it, but *you can only connect the dots looking backwards*.

Musically, it's a freer project than the previous ones, with a greater density of ideas: more work put into the mix, into guitar loops, and fewer conceptual restrictions.

Today I choose to upload it as it was saved, to pick up that path again. It's time to become a channel for those ideas again, and the first step is **accepting** them as they are. It's up to you to see if there's something there and complete the cycle.

More soon, as dano says: **always things**.
