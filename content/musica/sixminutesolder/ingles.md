Around 2021 I started accumulating an immense number of shelved tracks, loops and unfinished ideas. ***six minutes older*** came out of a simple constraint: every track had to last exactly *one minute*.

The idea was to give some of those fragments a place to exist instead of leaving them archived forever. The tracks are minimal and sparse, with fewer elements, more space and a somewhat naive, arcade-like sound. Most of the EP was built around experimenting with a 909 drum machine VST and simple electronic arrangements.

The **video and title** came together while I was structuring the EP, which also became a quick excuse to practice mixing. The number of archived tracks didn't decrease at all; I'd have to reiterate.
