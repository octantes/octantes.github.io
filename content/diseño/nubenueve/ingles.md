***nubenueve*** is a niche record label and digital storefront built around the physical experience of music: vinyl, cassette, CDs and the artwork that makes each release feel collectible and memorable.

Since the catalogue already carries the character, the brand identity and website needed a restrained visual language that introduced warmth and made browsing feel like flipping through a record collection.

### Album Art

The album covers needed to work as individual releases, each carrying the artist's concept, while still reading as part of one catalogue. They all explore a different visual direction, but shared internal references and layout keep the collection connected.

![Album Art](album-art.png)

### The Page

The website gives the catalogue room to be discovered through the covers first, with physical formats and release information following naturally in a slow-tempo to give each artwork time to be experienced.

The homepage has a presentation system that animates with the art from every release, built to grow over time as more artists and records are added in.
